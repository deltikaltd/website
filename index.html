Mission

Software that stays out of the way.

We design and ship products that help people and organisations work with more clarity: fewer unnecessary steps, information that reaches the right place, and tools that stay out of the way.

AI is part of how we build, not a slogan. We use it where it makes a product better — to understand context, connect the right things, and cut friction. The result still has to be simple enough to use without a manual.

We start with hiring, then grow that into an app ecosystem people can actually join.

Deltika is a product company, founded by three partners with that idea in mind. We will keep building.

How we work

What we hold on to.

  • Clarity

    Fewer steps, better signal

    Work gets harder when information is scattered and tools ask for too much. We aim for products that make the next step obvious.

  • Everyday use

    Not a demo

    If a feature only looks good on a slide, it does not belong in the product. We build for the hours people actually spend at work.

  • AI with a job

    Useful, or left out

    We use AI where it cuts friction — not as decoration. If it does not make the product simpler to use, we do not add it.

About

A small company, built around the word “why”.

Deltika LTD is a software company. We design tools that help people understand financial and property decisions — not only the score at the end of a model, but the path that produced it.

How we work

Most teams already have data. The harder part is trusting what a model does with it. We focus on software that makes that reasoning visible: which signals mattered, which ones did not, and what changes if the market moves.

That approach grew out of work on explainable models and on short-term rental analysis — comparing whether a property is stronger as a short stay or a longer let, and why.

We stay small on purpose. Projects are scoped around a real decision someone has to make, then turned into a tool they can keep using.
